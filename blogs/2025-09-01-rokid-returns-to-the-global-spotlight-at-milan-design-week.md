Following its impressive display at CES 2025, where Rokid impressed global viewers with its stunning applicability and comfort, Rokid is back in the global limelight, this time at Milan Design Week 2025. At the center of Rokid’s presentation in Milan is the Rokid AR Spatial , an advancement in AR eyewear that fully leverages the Max 2’s advantages—portability, visual clarity, and comfort—to create a comprehensive, immersive spatial computing experience. Located inside the ADI Design Museum, AR Spatial enthralled both visitors and design experts with its sophisticated multi-screen functionality
