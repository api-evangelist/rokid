Rokid has officially launched Rokid Glasses, the world's lightest full-function AI & AR smart glasses. The glasses are lightweight, AI-powered, and offer real-time translation, object recognition, navigation, transcription, notifications, and other AI-driven functions. Built on the Qualcomm AR1 platform, they strike a balance of style, performance, and industrial-grade reliability, making them ideal for everyday life.
