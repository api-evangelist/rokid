JAKARTA - Technology company Rokid officially introduced its latest AI smart glasses, Rokid Style, at the 2026 Consumer Electronics Show (CES). Unlike most smart glasses on the market, Rokid Style comes without a screen and carries a voice-first concept, with a focus on lightweight and practical everyday use. Rokid calls Rokid Style an ultra-light smart glasses with a weight of only 38.5 grams.
