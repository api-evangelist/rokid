Milan Design Week 2025 successfully concluded last week in Milan, drawing global visitors and design professionals to celebrate innovation, culture, and technology. Among the standout exhibits was Rokid AR Spatial , a pioneering AR product that garnered widespread attention throughout the event. As a leading Chinese enterprise in AR and AI technologies, Rokid introduced its latest smart glasses, Rokid AR Spatial, to the international design stage, specifically exhibiting under Milano Design Week China & Design.
